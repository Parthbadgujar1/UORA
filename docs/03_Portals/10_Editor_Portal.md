# 1. Overview

The Editor Portal is responsible for managing the complete editorial workflow of the journal.

Editors receive manuscript submissions, perform initial screening, assign reviewers, evaluate peer reviews, communicate with authors, and prepare accepted manuscripts for publication.

The portal is the operational core of the UORA Platform.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Editor Portal enables editors to

- Manage manuscript submissions
- Coordinate peer review
- Maintain publication quality
- Communicate with authors
- Manage reviewers
- Prepare journal issues
- Publish accepted research
