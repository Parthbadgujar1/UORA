# Author Portal

Version: 2.0

Authentication: Required

Role: Author

---

# 1. Overview

The Author Portal allows researchers to submit, manage, and track manuscripts throughout the publication lifecycle.

Every registered author has access to a personalized dashboard.

---

# 2. Objectives

Provide a simple manuscript submission experience.

Allow transparent tracking.

Enable communication with editors.

Maintain publication history.
