---

# 1. Overview

The Admin Portal is responsible for managing the complete UORA Platform.

Administrators oversee journals, users, editorial teams, website content, system configuration, reports, and platform operations.

Unlike editors, administrators manage the platform itself rather than individual manuscript workflows.
