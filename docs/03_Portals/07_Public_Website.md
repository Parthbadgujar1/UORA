Version: 2.0

---

# 1. Overview

The Public Website is the public-facing entry point of the UORA Platform.

It provides information about the organization, journals, publications, editorial boards, and published research while allowing visitors to explore academic content without authentication.

This website also serves as the gateway to all authenticated portals.
