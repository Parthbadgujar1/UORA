# UORA Platform - Project Summary

Version: 2.0
Status: Planning & Development
Product Type: Multi-Journal Academic Publishing Platform

---

# 1. Introduction

Universal Oneness Research Association (UORA) is a multidisciplinary academic publishing organization committed to promoting high-quality research across Science, Technology, Engineering, Management, Medical Sciences, Humanities, Arts, and other emerging disciplines.

To support this vision, UORA is developing a centralized digital publishing platform that manages the complete lifecycle of scholarly publishing—from manuscript submission to peer review, editorial management, publication, indexing, and public access.

Unlike a traditional journal website, UORA is designed as a scalable publishing ecosystem capable of hosting multiple independent journals under one unified platform.

---

# 2. Vision

Build a world-class publishing platform that enables researchers, editors, reviewers, and institutions to collaborate efficiently while maintaining the highest standards of academic publishing.

The platform should support multiple journals, multiple editorial teams, multiple user roles, and thousands of research articles without requiring architectural changes.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Currently, many academic journals rely on fragmented systems for manuscript submission, peer review, publication management, and communication.

Common problems include

- Multiple disconnected websites
- Manual paper tracking
- Email-based review process
- Difficult editorial management
- No centralized administration
- Limited scalability
- Inconsistent user experience

These challenges reduce operational efficiency and create unnecessary complexity for authors, reviewers, editors, and administrators.

---

# 4. Solution

The UORA Platform provides a unified academic publishing ecosystem where all stakeholders work through a single integrated platform.

The platform supports

- Public journal websites
- Author Portal
- Reviewer Portal
- Editor Portal
- Admin Portal

All connected to one backend and one database.

---

# 5. Product Objectives

The primary objectives of UORA are

• Publish high-quality peer-reviewed journals.

• Digitize the complete editorial workflow.

• Reduce manual administrative work.

• Provide transparency during manuscript processing.

• Support multiple journals from one backend.

• Enable scalable journal management.

• Improve researcher experience.

• Maintain ethical publishing standards.

# 6. Core Product

The platform consists of two major sections.

## Public Platform

Accessible without login.

Purpose

- Introduce UORA
- Display journals
- Browse articles
- View issues
- Search research
- Download published papers
- Contact organization

---

## Authenticated Platform

Accessible after login.

Role-based access.

Includes

- Author Portal

- Reviewer Portal

- Editor Portal

- Admin Portal

Each portal provides different functionality according to the user's role.
